**[0:00 – 0:05] — THE CHAMBER**

*Fade in from black.*
*A vast stone oracle chamber, torchlit, misted at the floor.*
*The altar — stone, ornate, ancient — glows softly at the far end.*
*The space breathes. Waiting.*

*TEXT OVERLAY — slow fade in, centered, Cinzel font:*
> *"The scroll hums in your hands..."*

---

**[0:05 – 0:13] — THE SCROLL RESPONDS**

*Close on the Scroll of Destiny — aged parchment, half-unrolled.*
*Three runes carved into the surface pulse with warm amber light.*
*Not glowing gems — carved angular marks, Elder Futhark style.*
*Each rune ignites in sequence, sending a ripple of light outward.*
*Between them, thematic descriptors in faint script — readable but barely.*
*The guild names are not visible. Only the runes and their descriptors.*

*TEXT OVERLAY — fades in after third rune:*
> *"Your signposts stir..."*

---

**[0:13 – 0:22] — THE ORACLE AWAKENS**

*The golden statue at the altar.*
*Still. Ancient. Watching from its stone pedestal.*
*A corona of amber-gold light builds slowly around it.*
*Angular runic marks carved in the stone floor begin to glow faintly.*
*Golden particles rise upward, dissolving into darkness.*
*The chamber trembles — barely. One breath of ancient power.*

*No text.*

---

**[0:22 – 0:31] — THE VISION**

*The world dissolves into abstraction.*
*Golden threads on deep void black — pulling, converging.*
*Brief glimpses flash through: silhouettes of human endeavor — a field,
a structure rising, hands at work — nothing literal, nothing labeled.*
*The threads tighten toward a point.*

*TEXT OVERLAY — appears mid-sequence:*
> *"The threads of your fate converge..."*

---

**[0:31 – 0:37] — CONVERGENCE**

*All threads pull toward a single blazing point.*
*From the light: the silhouette of an ancient tome.*
*Thick. Bound. A single rune glowing on the spine — amber.*
*The book hangs in the void, turning slowly.*
*The light intensifies—*

---

**[0:37 – 0:40] — THE SILENCE**

*CUT TO BLACK.*
*One beat of silence.*
*Book scroll scene begins.*

### IMG-02 — Scroll Close-Up

*This is the most important image in the sequence. It must reflect the actual
Scroll of Destiny design. Read these notes carefully before generating.*

**Key visual requirements:**
- The scroll is aged parchment, half-unrolled, shot in extreme close-up
- Three runes are carved/burned into the surface — they glow with amber light
- The runes are ANGULAR, CARVED MARKS in the Elder Futhark tradition —
  straight lines, stave-and-branch grammar, NO circles, NO gems, NO modern
  iconography. Think actual Norse rune carvings, not fantasy sigil art.
- Below each rune, faint script text is barely legible — these are the
  thematic descriptors ("Cycle of the Living Land", "Emanation of Wholeness",
  "Axis of Discovery" or similar). They should be visible but secondary.
- Guild names are NOT present. The scroll does not name the guilds at this
  stage of the game.
- The parchment background: warm cream-tan, aged with use, slight wear at
  edges, calligraphic text faintly visible in the background as texture
- Lighting comes from within the runes themselves — amber-gold glow emanating
  from the carved marks, rippling outward softly
- Overall mood: sacred, personal, prophetic

## PART 7 — NOTES FOR THE EDIT SESSION

**On IMG-02 specifically:** This image carries the most narrative weight.
If the runes don't read as angular carved marks, regenerate before editing.
The three runes should look like they belong on the same document as the
in-game Scroll of Destiny. If you can reference the actual Scroll mockup
image during generation, do so.

**On the music:** If you can't generate or commission AUD-02, a workable
substitute is any dark orchestral track from a royalty-free library
(Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Pixabay) in the 40-50 second range that has
a clear build-to-climax-to-silence structure. Layer AUD-01 ambient underneath
at -12db regardless.

**On timing:** The 40-second runtime is intentional — it matches the intro
cinematic length and creates a sense of structural symmetry in the game.
Don't cut it shorter. The pacing should feel slow and deliberate. Students
should feel the weight of what is happening to their character.

**On the book scroll scene handoff:** The cinematic ends on pure black.
The React `OracleProphecyReveal.tsx` component takes over immediately.
No fade, no transition — the black frame is shared. The scroll-unfurl
sound effect (`1o1DueEFC43WK7CDV3_EQEX1lCTHmtfGg`) fires as the first
book appears.
