# Master Scribe Voice Brief

## Creative Direction

Create an original elderly female voice for the Master Scribe.

The voice should be:

- Low contralto
- Weathered and gently scratchy
- Dry, smoky, and textured without becoming hard to understand
- Patient and measured
- Warm beneath a stern surface
- Calmly authoritative rather than theatrical
- Mythic, but still believable as a mentor speaking directly to a student

Avoid:

- Imitating a specific actor or public figure
- A dramatic trailer-announcer performance
- An exaggerated witch voice
- Heavy whispering
- Rasp so strong that instructional language becomes unclear
- Excessively slow pacing

## ElevenLabs Voice Design Prompt

An elderly woman with a low contralto voice and a naturally weathered, gently gravelly texture. Her delivery is
measured, patient, and precise, with quiet authority and restrained warmth. She sounds like a lifelong keeper of
records who has guided many young travelers. The voice is mythic and memorable but never theatrical, exaggerated, or
sinister. Clear American English, excellent intelligibility, subtle dry rasp, calm pacing.

## Audition Script

Generate only these lines while choosing the voice:

1. "Traveler... At last."
2. "Before the Scroll can guide you, you must first discover who you are."
3. "Not every Echo is defeated by force. Some fade only when met with understanding."
4. "The Scroll has awakened. Wait..."
5. "They are not your destiny. They are not a verdict. They are the first roads asking to be examined."

## Approval Checklist

- The voice remains clear on the words "Foretold Signposts" and "Mirror of Maia."
- The rasp sounds natural across both short and long lines.
- Short lines do not sound clipped or overly dramatic.
- Instructional lines remain easy for students to follow.
- The performance sounds distinct from the intro narrator and Oracle.
- The voice can sustain 68 Master Scribe clips without becoming tiring.

## After Approval

Copy the approved voice ID into the local environment as:

```powershell
$env:ELEVENLABS_MASTER_SCRIBE_VOICE_ID = "approved voice id"
```

Do not generate the full dialogue batch until this audition is approved.
