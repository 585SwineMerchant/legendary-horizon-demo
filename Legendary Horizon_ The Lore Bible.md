# **📜 Legendary Horizon: The Lore Bible**

**Project Goal:** To transform a middle school career decision-making unit into a cohesive, gamified "Hero’s Journey" where students (Travelers) move from self-discovery to real-world career planning.

## **📜 The Narrative Arc: A Hero’s Ascension**

### **Act I: The Awakening (The Master Scribe & The Mirror)**

**Narrative Goal:** Character Creation and the binding of the Scroll of Destiny.

* **Encounter: The Master Scribe:** Travelers meet **The Master Scribe**. The Scribe explains that no one can walk a path they do not first see within themselves.  
* **The Ritual of Reflection:** The Scribe sends the Traveler through the **Mirror of Maia** (Maia Learning assessments). This is a mandatory ritual to "reveal the soul's current frequency."  
* **The Scribe’s Survey:** Upon exiting the Mirror, the Master Scribe conducts a post-reflection survey. These answers are used to inscribe the **Scroll of Destiny**, which acts as the Traveler’s Character Sheet and includes:  
  1. **Base Stats:** Calculated RIASEC/Holland Codes.  
  2. **Rune Slots:** Identifying specific Career Interests and Values.  
  3. **The 3 Foretold Signposts:** The three specific Realms (Career Clusters) calling to the Traveler.  
  4. **The Traveler’s Manifest (Inception):** An interactive form generated from the Scribe's survey. This is a living document that travelers will eventually "copy and paste" into the official NYS Career Plan.

### **Act II: The Divination (The Rituals of Research)**

**Narrative Goal:** Learning the "Rituals of Research" and unlocking tools.

* **The Oracle of Opportunity:** Grants a **Prophecy** (a random career) to take to the **Vault of Runes** (ONetOnline).  
* **The Comparison Ledger:** Successfully navigating the Vault unlocks the **Comparison Ledger**. This magical book allows Travelers to practice recording data for the journey ahead.

### **Act III: Mapping the World (The Fog of the Unknown)**

**Narrative Goal:** Exploration, Discovery, and Live Recording.

* **The Great Clearing:** Armed with their **Magical Map** and **Comparison Ledger**, Travelers enter the world gathering information and clearing **The Fog of the Unknown**.  
* **Shattering the Fog:** Travelers move their avatars to waypoints on the map. They use the **Comparison Ledger** to research careers specifically within their **3 Foretold Signposts**, though they may explore all 16 Realms.

### **Act IV: The Guild Trials (The Chosen Path)**

**Narrative Goal:** Commitment, Professionalism, and Final Inscription.

* **Selecting the True Path:** The Traveler chooses one specific career from their Ledger—their **True Path**.  
* **🛡️ The Trial of Tongues:** The Traveler journeys to a Guild HQ to face the **NPC Guild Manager**.  
  1. **The Enrollment Rune:** Completing a **Job Application Form** to prove their skills.  
  2. **The Interview & Artificer’s Ethics:** An AI-powered encounter testing tone, professionalism, and soft-skill "Charisma Checks" (Integrity, Punctuality, etc.).  
* **The Final Inscription:** Upon Guild acceptance, the Traveler returns to **The Master Scribe** . They finalize the **Traveler's Manifest**, filling in the final deep-dive research for their True Path.  
* **The Great Transcription:** With the Manifest complete, the Scribe guides the Traveler in "transcribing" (copy-pasting) their data into the official **NYS Career Plan** on Maia Learning.

### **Final Act: The Ascension (The Grand Council)**

**Narrative Goal:** Mastery and Communal Revelation.

* **The Chronicle of the Horizon:** Travelers produce a master record of their journey—a **Google Slides** presentation titled their "Chronicle."  
* **The Grand Council Career Fair:** A formal class event where Travelers present their **Chronicle of the Horizon** to their peers and the High Council (the teacher).  
* **The Victory:** Proving they possess the plan to navigate the world beyond the classroom.

## **🏔️ The Post-Game & Epilogue**

* **The Legendary Horizon:** A concept representing the Traveler's distant future—a goal that remains on the horizon to keep them moving forward.  
* **The Epilogue (Expansion Pack Setup):**  
  * **The Course Selection Ritual:** Travelers interact with the **Greece Central Schools Course Selection Guide**.  
  * **The 7th Grade Setup:** Choosing the "spells" (classes) needed for their 7th-grade Expansion Pack.
