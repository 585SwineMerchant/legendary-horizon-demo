Quest Phase I

Legendary Horizon: The RPG

In Legendary Horizon, the conventional classroom career survey is reconceptualized. Middle schoolers are not filling out spreadsheet lists or reading flat manuals. They are entering a fantasy world where customized landscapes are designed around Career and Technical Education fields.

Direct Digital Integrations

Maia Learning Sync

Syncs quest completions directly to personal career interest profile assessments.

Live API Feeds

Connects interactive NPC dialogue to real-time O*NET and CareerOneStop wage indices.

NYS Career Plan Alignment

Converts state compliance checklist mandates into a dynamic Hero's Logbook that updates as students unlock new regions of the map.

Quest Phase II

Underlying Learning Theories

1. Constructivist Learning

Active Building

Students act as constructors of their own knowledge. By exploring realms like the Etheric Nexus or Aurora Apothecary, they construct career schemas internally rather than passively reviewing descriptions.

2. Experiential Learning

Concrete Play

The Zelda-style gameplay cycle provides immediate interaction, then moves learners into reflection as they adjust character attributes based on real career values.

3. Self-Determination

Intrinsic Flow

Gamification supports autonomy, competence, and relatedness: students choose paths, earn badges, and discuss quest strategies with peers.

Quest Phase III

Key Pedagogy Takeaways

01

The Character Creation Metaphor Middle school and early high school students are at the character creation phase of their life games. Framing career exploration this way reduces anxiety and transforms a state requirement into an empowering story.

02

Authentic Interdisciplinarity This experience bridges compliance standards and state-of-the-art tech by giving students visual, spatial, and gamified autonomy over their interface.

03

Iterative Design and Collaboration Peer feedback in the CTE graduate program helped simple interactive tools scale into a cohesive curricular system that supports long-term student success.
