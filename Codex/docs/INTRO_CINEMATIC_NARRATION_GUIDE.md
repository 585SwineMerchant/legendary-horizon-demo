# Intro Cinematic Narration Guide

## Recommendation

Use recorded narration files for the Corey leadership demo. Browser text-to-speech is useful for testing, but it is too inconsistent for a high-stakes leadership walkthrough.

The app is now wired to play one optional MP3 per scene from:

`Codex/frontend/public/assets/intro/audio/`

## Target Runtime

Aim for 75 to 100 seconds total.

The opener should feel polished but quick. Corey should understand the instructional model before the playable slice begins.

## Voice Direction

- Calm, confident, mentor-like.
- More school-leadership presentation than fantasy trailer.
- Avoid overacting. The “wow” should come from clarity, polish, and coherence.
- Pace should be slightly slower than normal speech, with short pauses after “Maia Learning,” “Scroll of Destiny,” and “NYS Career Plan.”

## Scene Scripts

### 1. The Mirror of Maia

Every journey begins with a mirror. In Legendary Horizon, students first step into Maia Learning to complete the work that grounds the adventure: interests, recommendations, and early career signals.

Expected file:

`scene_1_awakening.mp3`

### 2. The Character Sheet

Those Maia results are not replaced by the game. They are interpreted by the teacher and translated into game-ready values: base stats, opening strengths, and the first paths worth investigating.

Expected file:

`scene_2_base_stats.mp3`

### 3. The Realm Atlas

The world opens into career-cluster realms. Students explore, research, compare, and collect evidence so the choices in Maia become more meaningful than a list on a screen.

Expected file:

`scene_3_library.mp3`

### 4. The Scroll of Destiny

The Scroll of Destiny turns reflection into a guided plan: a manifest, foretold signposts, and a clearer reason to investigate careers before choosing a direction.

Expected file:

`scene_4_scroll.mp3`

### 5. The Comparison Trials

Students then compare real careers: requirements, training, earnings, lifestyle fit, and the evidence behind each possible path.

Expected file:

`scene_5_comparison.mp3`

### 6. Return to Maia

After exploration, students return to Maia and the NYS Career Plan with stronger language, sharper questions, and a better understanding of why the plan matters.

Expected file:

`scene_6_return_to_maia.mp3`

## Practical Production Options

### Best personal option

Record the narration yourself in a quiet room. Use a phone or computer microphone, then trim silence at the beginning and end of each clip. This has the most authentic educator feel.

### Best AI option

Use an AI voice tool to export one MP3 per scene. Choose a warm adult narrator voice, not a dramatic trailer voice. Keep the output dry, with no music baked in, so we can balance audio later.

### Testing option

Use browser text-to-speech only to test pacing. Do not rely on it for the Corey demo.

## Music and Sound

Keep music subtle and separate from narration if possible. If we add music, it should sit under the voice at low volume and never compete with the instructional message.
